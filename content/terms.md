## What Manazil is
Manazil is an app for reading and listening to the Quran, with prayer times, a Qibla compass and reminders. It is a tool to support your worship. It does not give religious rulings. For a question of religion, ask a qualified scholar.

## Prayer times, Qibla and reminders
- Prayer times follow published tables or are calculated from your location. They can differ by a minute or two from your local masjid. If you need certainty, check with your masjid.
- The Qibla direction depends on your phone's location and compass sensor. Some phones have no compass sensor, and Manazil then shows the bearing as a number only.
- Reminders depend on your phone's settings, such as notification permission, battery saving and Do Not Disturb. A reminder may arrive late or not at all.

## Verse Finder
Speech recognition can get a verse wrong. Always check the verse you are shown against the Mushaf.
